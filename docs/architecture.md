## Simulator

The `backend/sim` package owns validated configuration, a warehouse grid of free cells, aisles, walls, and charging stations, and the robot, job, and fault state. `New` validates and clones configuration. Robots hold integer battery percentages; jobs progress from pending through assigned and in-progress to done, retaining assignment history.

Phase 2 foundations add `WarehouseConfig`: a 20×12 grid with 104 shelf walls and three eight-cell aisles at columns 4, 9, and 14, leaving 134 eligible job cells. `DefaultConfig` retains Phase 1 geometry with AutoCharge=false; WarehouseConfig enables AutoCharge. V12 checks orthogonal connectivity through free/aisle cells and requires adjacent access for every station; station starts remain allowed, but stations cannot connect components. V13 permits at most one initial robot per aisle. V14 validates replanning, blocking, reserve battery, and enabled automatic-charge thresholds.

Ticks advance deterministically using a per-Sim seeded RNG for job generation. Pass A processes all robots in numeric ID order: decrement countdowns, apply offline expiry before other behavior, handle charging, and drain idle batteries. Pass B uses scheduler.Assign for pending jobs in numeric ID order, selecting the nearest eligible idle robot by parked-obstacle A* distance, with numeric robot IDs breaking ties. Both route legs must be reachable, and battery must meet MinAssignBattery and cover their combined movement cost plus ReserveBattery. With AutoCharge enabled, scheduler JobInfo.ExitCost also budgets the parked-obstacle distance from delivery to a currently available charging station; -1 rejects the job. AutoCharge=false uses ExitCost=0 and retains legacy behavior. A robot finishing charging is excluded for the whole tick. Pass C, when AutoCharge is enabled, selects charging trips for remaining idle robots below ChargeBelow. Pass D moves job and charging trips in numeric ID order.

Movement follows private A* routes, at most one cell per tick, with walls impassable and stations excluded from transit. Robots reject occupied cells or aisles held by another robot. Actual movement drains battery, including arrival steps; idle robots drain periodically. SetCharging requires an idle, job-free robot already at a station, checking occupied before reserved-by-another before busy/position errors. Charge is capped at 100, and completion frees the station without dispatching on that tick. Dispatch distance caches last only one pass and use map lookups without map iteration for selection.

Autonomous charging processes eligible idle, jobless robots by battery ascending, then numeric ID. Each immediately claims the nearest free reachable station by parked-obstacle A* distance, breaking ties by numeric station ID. Free means unoccupied, unreserved, and physically empty, including the candidate's own position. New trips must retain at least one battery unit on arrival. An eligible robot already on an unoccupied/unreserved station begins charging through the existing rule instead. Nonzero trips reserve the station and move with no job using existing navigation/wait/replan rules. Arrival atomically clears reservation, sets occupancy/status, and resets waiting; gain begins next tick. The existing zero-battery arrival exception remains a safety net for a pre-existing trip, not an admission budget. Depletion before arrival faults the robot and releases its reservation; offline charging occupants retain their station. Charging still runs to 100 and completion skips the rest of that tick. Queued/stranded classification is derived without new snapshot fields.

With AutoCharge enabled, ReserveBattery is the single assignment-admission contingency and must be in [5,25]. Subsequent job routes budget remaining pickup/delivery and charging exit with a floor of one battery unit, spending that contingency rather than adding it again. Relocation replacements also budget their exit; charging replacements retain one on arrival. Post-assignment exit estimates keep parked obstacles en route but ignore occupancy/reservation at the destination station, as approved for Step 1; assignment still requires a currently free station. Pickup movement is charged exactly once before planning delivery. The existing exact-zero charging-arrival safety net remains unchanged for pre-existing trips. Unaffordable replacement routes preserve the previous route, job, and reservation; waiting consumes no battery. Future legs and exits use parked-obstacle estimates and do not reserve or guarantee future station availability. AutoCharge=false retains legacy job-route behavior.

Initial pickup/delivery routes avoid offline, faulted, and charging robots and their aisles. Replanning avoids all other robots and occupied aisles, exempting the planning robot's own cell and aisle. A blocked step stays stationary without battery drain, increments waitTicks once, and replans at each ReplanAfter multiple. Successful replanning replaces the route for the next tick without resetting waiting; failed replanning retains the old route. BlockedAfter sets blocked. Movement or transition to a non-moving status clears waiting. There is no automatic deadlock resolution or traffic-generated fault history.

| Call class | Operations | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DYNAMICS | `Tick`, including background dispatch | Background simulation, not an operator command |
| READ | `Snapshot`, `CellAt` | Snapshot owns deep copies; grid lookup is read-only |
| READ | `PlanRoute`, `AisleOccupant`, `OccupiedAislesOnRoute` | Static A* routing and physical aisle occupancy; no state mutation |
| DRIVER | `InjectLowBattery`, `InjectUnresponsive`, `InjectAisleConflict` | Trusted tests, evaluations, and scenario triggers only; never exposed to the agent |
| COMMAND | `SetCharging` | Future operator execution requires validation and approval |

`Reader` exposes the READ operations. `Commander` embeds Reader and adds SetCharging; `Driver` embeds Reader and adds the three trusted injectors. Compile-time checks enforce Sim conformance. The exported-method guard requires every exported Sim method to match one of these role interfaces, except Tick; import and randomness restrictions remain enforced.

Snapshots include robot waitTicks/blocked, station reservedBy, and an ordered aisles array with zero-based IDs. Moving without a job requires enabled AutoCharge and a valid station reservation (I6); reservations must belong to charging travelers, be unique per robot, and never coexist with occupancy (I16). Aisle occupancy derives from current robot positions within the configured Y range and selects the lowest numeric robot ID if inconsistent input contains multiple occupants. Tick refreshes stored aisle records after movement; Snapshot and occupancy READs recompute without mutating state. PlanRoute adapts Config to routing.Grid, permits station endpoints, excludes station transit, and ignores robots. Route occupancy results contain distinct ascending aisle IDs whose selected occupant differs from the caller. Other traffic invariants enforce wall avoidance (I2), unique cells (I12), exclusive aisles (I13), waiting consistency (I14), and physical occupancy snapshots (I15).

Background dispatch must not become a bypass around future safety validation. Phase 3 owns the validator, executor, operator approval, auditing, and reassignment under the frozen safety rules. Sim is not goroutine-safe; Phase 3 will serialize access. Agent access will use a later GraphQL API, never direct simulator imports.

Unresponsive countdowns run before other robot behavior, even while faulted. Expiry makes the robot offline, overriding faulted without another movement, drain, or charging step. Offline robots retain their jobs and occupied stations, while interrupted charge trips release reservations. Status transitions, scheduling, and autonomous charging create no fault records; successful trusted injections do.

Pass C2 now implements SA1 protected-cell evacuation when Recovery is enabled. Each simulator caches station cells, their walkable orthogonal access cells, and all aisle cells from its cloned configuration: 30 cells in WarehouseConfig. Eligible idle, jobless, non-skipped robots are processed numerically and choose a dynamically reachable unoccupied free target outside this set by route length, then Y/X. Targets of in-flight trips are excluded and each new destination is claimed immediately. Admission budgets relocation plus the shortest charging exit and retains one battery unit. The approved exit model uses parked obstacles en route but ignores occupancy/reservation at the destination station. Rejected evacuation retries on later ticks. Private relocation navigation has no station reservation; arrival returns to idle without a hold. Assigned/charging robots are excluded, and completed chargers can evacuate no earlier than the next tick. Conditional aisle-mouth expansion is not enabled because Step 1 diagnostics did not identify the required root-cause trigger. No simulator JSON fields or exported methods are added. RecoverAfter=15, HoldTicks=3, reserve values, and existing replan/retreat policies remain unchanged in Step 1.

R3 adds Pass E after movement, gated by Recovery and any wait reaching RecoverAfter. Numeric-ID dependency traversal prefers the occupant of the next required cell, otherwise the owner of the aisle being entered. Only healthy cycles qualify. Consider cycle members in descending numeric order, excluding robots already moved, holding, or inside aisles. Retreat neighbors are right/left/down/up; destinations must be unoccupied free cells outside aisles and stations, excluding other cycle members' next cells. Skip energy-infeasible members and select the first feasible retreat. Charge the retreat once, then budget the complete remaining mission/trip and charging exit (unless already charging-bound), retaining one battery unit using parked-obstacle estimates. Preserve jobs, reservations, and relocation ownership. A successful retreat stores a private route and holds for the next HoldTicks full ticks without drain or movement; countdown expiry still takes precedence. The following tick attempts dynamic replanning; failure retains the route/trip and waits without drain. At most ten retreats per robot are allowed in any 200 consecutive ticks, retaining history across mission/status transitions. Private counters/history are included in test invariants and diagnostic replay, not Snapshot JSON.

The standalone `cmd/simrun` CLI emits only the final indented Snapshot JSON. `-layout default|warehouse` selects the configuration, defaults to `default`, and rejects unknown layouts with exit code 2. Golden snapshot tests, static import/RNG guards, and invariant checks across seeded injection and charging scenarios verify reproducibility and state consistency. Map iteration that could affect determinism remains subject to tests and source review.
